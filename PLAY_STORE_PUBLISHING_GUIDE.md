# 📱 Google Play Store Public Publishing Guide
## [BOR] Smart Fridge & Culinary Assistant

This guide explains step-by-step how to publish this application to the **public Google Play Store** as an Android application using **Trusted Web Activity (TWA)** — the official standard recommended by Google for Progressive Web Apps.

---

## ⚡ Option A: Instant 1-Click Publishing with PWABuilder (Recommended)

1. **Visit PWABuilder**:
   Open [https://www.pwabuilder.com](https://www.pwabuilder.com) in your browser.
2. **Enter App URL**:
   Paste your deployed live application URL (e.g., your Cloud Run or Firebase Hosting URL) and click **Start**.
3. **Review PWA Score**:
   The manifest, high-resolution icons (192px, 512px, maskable), and service workers are already fully compliant and configured.
4. **Generate Android Package**:
   Click **"Package for Stores"** -> choose **"Google Play"**.
   - Package ID: `app.smartfridge.culinary.twa`
   - App Name: `Smart Fridge & Culinary Assistant`
   - Launcher Name: `SmartFridge`
   - Version: `1.0.0`
5. **Download the Signed Bundle**:
   Click **"Generate Bundle"** to download the `app-release.aab` file and the signing key credentials.

---

## 🛠️ Option B: Using Google's Official Bubblewrap CLI

Bubblewrap is Google's official Node.js command-line tool for generating production-ready Android App Bundles:

```bash
# 1. Install Google Bubblewrap CLI
npm install -g @bubblewrap/cli

# 2. Initialize from Web Manifest
bubblewrap init --manifest https://<YOUR-APP-DOMAIN>/manifest.webmanifest

# 3. Build signed Android App Bundle (.aab)
bubblewrap build
```

This generates `app-release-bundle.aab` ready for upload to Google Play Console.

## 🏪 Google Play Console Submission Steps

### 1. Create Developer Account
- Go to [Google Play Console](https://play.google.com/console).
- Sign in with your Google Account ($25 one-time registration fee).

### 2. Create New App
- Click **"Create app"**.
- **App name**: `[BOR] Smart Fridge & Culinary Assistant`
- **Default language**: English (United States)
- **App or game**: App
- **Free or paid**: Free
- Accept Declarations & terms.

### 3. Store Listing Assets (Provided in this project)
- **Short description** (max 80 chars):
  `Snap your fridge, detect ingredients with AI, cook hands-free with voice guides.`
- **Full description**:
  `[BOR] Smart Fridge is your ultimate kitchen companion. Snap a photo of your fridge or pantry to automatically detect ingredients using Gemini AI vision. Filter recipes by dietary goals (vegetarian, keto, high-protein, gluten-free), cook step-by-step hands-free with voice control and speech read-aloud, and manage your culinary inventory seamlessly.`
- **App Icon**: Use `public/pwa-512x512.png` (512x512 px, 32-bit PNG).
- **Feature Graphic**: Use `public/play-feature-graphic.png` (1024x500 px, PNG).
- **Category**: Food & Drink.

### 4. Upload Android App Bundle (.aab)
- In Google Play Console sidebar, go to **Testing** -> **Internal testing** (or **Production**).
- Click **"Create new release"**.
- Drag and drop your `app-release.aab` file generated by PWABuilder or Bubblewrap.
- Enter Release notes: `Initial public release of [BOR] Smart Fridge & Culinary Assistant`.
- Click **Save** and **Review release**.

### 5. Link Digital Asset Links (Full-screen verification)
To ensure the app opens seamlessly without a browser address bar:
1. In Google Play Console, go to **Setup** -> **App signing**.
2. Copy the **SHA-256 certificate fingerprint** of the App Signing key.
3. Update `public/.well-known/assetlinks.json`:
```json
[
  {
    "relation": ["delegate_permission/common.handle_all_urls"],
    "target": {
      "namespace": "android_app",
      "package_name": "app.smartfridge.culinary.twa",
      "sha256_cert_fingerprints": [
        "<PASTE-YOUR-GOOGLE-PLAY-SHA256-FINGERPRINT-HERE>"
      ]
    }
  }
]
```
4. Deploy the update so Google Play verifies domain ownership.

### 6. Submit for Review
- Complete the Content Rating questionnaire (Everyone / PEGI 3).
- Provide Privacy Policy URL.
- Submit the release for review! Google typically approves in 24-48 hours.
